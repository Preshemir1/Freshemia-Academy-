Why Freshemia

A serious institution, not a training centre.

Freshemia Academy operates a formal admissions process, documented academic records and verifiable student credentials — the same discipline you would expect from any established college.

  • Documented admissions policy with an independent review board.
  • Every admission letter carries a unique admission number and QR code for third-party verification.
  • Student identity cards issued from the portal, ready for campus access.
  • Records retained securely; passport data handled under our privacy policy.
“We admit students the way industry hires: verifying credentials, assessing readiness, and issuing records that can be trusted by employers.”
— Office of the Registrar, Freshemia Academy

Entry requirements

Minimum of five O'level credits including English Language and Mathematics for diploma programmes; three credits for the professional certificate. Mature applicants may be assessed on experience.
